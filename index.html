ProfileSync Privacy Policy

ProfileSync backs up website logins and cookies from your browser to your own backup server.

  • What is collected: login credentials (usernames and passwords) that your browser fills in on login pages, and browser cookies.
  • How it is used: solely to create and restore your backups.
  • Where it goes: sent over an encrypted connection to the backup service and stored on your backup server.
  • Sharing: never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising or unrelated purposes.
